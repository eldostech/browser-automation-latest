Create a project

Ready v2

Signs in, opens the new-project form, and files one project per row of the dataset.

Setup

Runs once for the whole batch, not once per row
4 steps
s4 Navigate uat.schemora.ai/login
s6 Fill role=textbox name="Email" secret.email
s9 Fill role=textbox name="Password" secret.password
s10 Click role=button name="Sign In"

Per row

Repeated for every row of the dataset
15 steps
s13 Fill role=textbox name="Legacy CRM to…" input.project_name
s15 Fill role=textbox name="Brief description" input.description
s16 Select role=combobox [0] input.source
s17 Select role=combobox [1] input.target
Show all 15 steps
